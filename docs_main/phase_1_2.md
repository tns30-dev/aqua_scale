# Sprint Backlog

The sprint backlog breaks the product backlog into one-week increments. Completed sprints are listed with their main backlog items and expected outcomes. Planned ML and agentic sprints are included as upcoming backlog items, but they are not counted as completed work.

| Sprint | Backlog Items | Status | Expected Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sprint 1 | Product objective, target users, business problem, platform scope | Completed | The product direction is clear: AquaShield supports aquaculture monitoring, treatment evidence, and operational decision-making. |
| Sprint 2 | Epics, user stories, use cases, architecture constraints | Completed | The product backlog is organized around farmers, researchers, product owners, business users, and administrators. |
| Sprint 3 | Data ownership, project structure, pond structure, cycle structure, sensor-reading context, treatment context | Completed | The platform has a clear domain model for aquaculture operations and evidence tracking. |
| Sprint 4 | Software design for critical user stories, interaction flow, platform responsibility boundaries | Completed | The solution design explains how core user stories are supported across the platform. |
| Sprint 5 | Secure access, user profile, project creation, profile setup, pond setup, parameter threshold setup | Completed | Users can manage the core farm and project foundation needed for monitoring. |
| Sprint 6 | Current water-quality dashboard, digital twin view, historical visualisation, treatment record capture | Completed | Farmers and researchers can observe pond conditions and begin connecting treatments with readings. |
| Sprint 7 | Alert generation, alert acknowledgement, notification readiness, traceability records | Completed | Unsafe readings can trigger action, and operational responses can be retained for review. |
| Sprint 8 | Pond comparison, summary views, reporting views, treatment-effectiveness comparison | Completed | Users can compare ponds and evaluate operational or product-effectiveness evidence. |
| Sprint 9 | Test cases, review preparation, security checks, deployment readiness, presentation evidence | Completed | The completed increment can be validated, demonstrated, and explained in a professional report. |
| Sprint 10 | ML water-quality prediction | Planned | Predict upcoming water-quality conditions using historical pond readings, cycles, treatments, and profile context. |
| Sprint 11 | Agentic alert explanation | Planned | Explain critical alerts in user-friendly operational language. |
| Sprint 12 | Agentic report assistant | Planned | Summarize pond performance, treatment evidence, and comparison results. |
| Sprint 13 | Agentic grounded Q&A | Planned | Answer questions using available project, pond, treatment, and report records. |
| Sprint 14 | Agentic human-reviewed recommendation | Planned | Suggest operational actions while keeping final judgement under human control. |

## Sprint Backlog Summary

The completed sprint backlog establishes the product foundation before adding intelligence features. The first nine sprints cover product definition, backlog design, data design, platform design, user/project/pond management, monitoring, treatment records, alerts, comparison, reporting, quality checks, and review evidence. Sprint 10 introduces ML prediction after the historical data foundation exists. Sprints 11 to 14 split the agentic work into four clear use cases: alert explanation, report assistant, grounded Q&A, and human-reviewed recommendation.
