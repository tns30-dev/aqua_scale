# Product/Platform Roadmap

AquaShield is an aquaculture decision-support platform designed to help farms, researchers, and aquaculture product businesses monitor pond conditions, evaluate treatment outcomes, and make data-backed operational decisions. Although the visible starting point of the product is water-quality monitoring, the platform is not limited to displaying sensor readings. Its broader purpose is to support aquaculture operations through pond-level digital twins, real-time water-quality tracking, energy and operational efficiency monitoring, pond-to-pond comparison, alerts, reporting, and treatment-effectiveness analysis. This is especially important for an aquaculture startup or product company because the business is not only selling a biological or treatment product; it also needs measurable evidence that the product improves pond conditions, reduces risk, lowers operating cost, or increases farming efficiency. To provide that evidence, AquaShield must collect reliable sensor data, organize it by project, pond, cycle, treatment, and profile type, and convert it into dashboards and analytics that different stakeholders can use.

The platform is designed around multiple user groups. Farmers and farm managers need a practical view of pond health, early warnings when parameters move outside safe ranges, comparison between ponds, and evidence of whether a treatment reduces problems such as excess nitrogen or unstable water conditions. Researchers and product developers need structured historical data, visual dashboards, comparison tools, and treatment-effectiveness records so they can evaluate whether their aquaculture product works under real farming conditions. Business owners and platform administrators need a scalable platform that can support different farm profiles, multiple projects, different users, configurable feature access, and consistent reporting across farm operations. This platform mindset changes AquaShield from a simple monitoring dashboard into a business tool for validating aquaculture products and improving farm operations.

The product roadmap begins with the operational platform foundation. The first roadmap area is user and access management so each stakeholder can use the platform according to their role. The second roadmap area is project, profile, pond, and cycle management so aquaculture data is organized around the real structure of farm work. The third roadmap area is sensor registration and water-quality monitoring so readings can be connected to the correct pond context. The fourth roadmap area is digital twin visualisation, historical charts, pond comparison, alerts, and notification workflows so users can understand both current pond status and historical operating patterns. The fifth roadmap area is treatment tracking and treatment-effectiveness reporting so the platform can support business evidence for aquaculture products. This roadmap keeps the Phase 0 scope focused on a practical product foundation: collect reliable pond data, organize it meaningfully, alert users when conditions change, and turn farm activity into evidence that farmers, researchers, and business owners can trust.
