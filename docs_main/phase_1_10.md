# Product/Platform Roadmap Updated

The AquaShield roadmap was updated after the core monitoring, alerting, comparison, and reporting foundation became clear. The Phase 0 roadmap focused on proving that the platform could collect pond data, organize it by farm context, surface operational issues, and produce evidence for treatment effectiveness. The updated Phase 1 roadmap keeps that product direction, but expands the platform from descriptive decision support into predictive and assisted decision support. This means AquaShield is no longer positioned only as a monitoring and reporting tool; it becomes a platform that can help farmers, researchers, and business owners understand what is happening, what may happen next, and what actions should be reviewed.

The updated roadmap introduces ML water-quality prediction only after the platform has enough structured pond, cycle, sensor, treatment, and historical reading data. Agentic Decision Support is placed after ML prediction because the assistant should not operate as a generic chatbot. It should explain alerts, summarize reports, answer grounded platform questions, and propose human-reviewed recommendations based on trusted AquaShield records.

## Roadmap Rationale

The completed roadmap stages establish the information foundation needed for advanced decision support. Farmers need reliable monitoring and alerting before they can trust predictions. Researchers and product developers need historical readings, treatment records, and comparison results before they can evaluate product performance. Business owners need reports and traceable evidence before the platform can support customer-facing product claims.

For that reason, the Phase 1 roadmap adds ML and Agentic capability as product extensions, not as replacements for the monitoring platform. ML prediction uses the structured platform records to estimate upcoming water-quality conditions. Agentic Decision Support uses the same trusted records to explain alerts, summarize evidence, answer platform questions, and propose actions for human review. This keeps the product direction practical: AquaShield first captures and organizes aquaculture data, then uses that data to support prediction, explanation, reporting, and human-reviewed operational decisions.
