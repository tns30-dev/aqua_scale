# Sprint Review

The sprint review summarizes the completed work from Sprint 1 to Sprint 9. It does not review Sprint 10 to Sprint 14 because the ML and agentic increments have not been executed yet.

| Sprint | Review Area | Review Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Sprint 1 | Product discovery and platform direction | The product objective, stakeholders, business value, and platform scope were clarified. |
| Sprint 2 | Product backlog and use-case design | The backlog was organized into epics and user stories for aquaculture monitoring, treatment evidence, alerts, reporting, and decision support. |
| Sprint 3 | Data and domain design | The main project, pond, cycle, sensor, reading, treatment, alert, and reporting concepts were defined. |
| Sprint 4 | Architecture and software design | The solution design and critical user-story flow were clarified. |
| Sprint 5 | User, project, profile, and pond foundation | The core setup flow for users, projects, profiles, ponds, parameters, and thresholds was completed. |
| Sprint 6 | Monitoring, digital twin, visualisation, and treatment records | Users can observe pond conditions, view historical trends, and capture treatment records. |
| Sprint 7 | Alerts, notification flow, and traceability | Unsafe readings can be surfaced as alerts, responses can be acknowledged, and operational history can be retained. |
| Sprint 8 | Pond comparison and reporting | Users can compare ponds and review evidence for operational and product-effectiveness decisions. |
| Sprint 9 | Quality, security, deployment, and presentation evidence | The completed increment was validated and prepared for demonstration and reporting. |

## Review Summary

The completed sprints produced a coherent AquaShield platform foundation. The work supports farmers by improving pond visibility and response to water-quality risk, supports researchers by organizing historical data and treatment evidence, and supports business owners by turning product performance into measurable reporting. The planned ML and agentic sprints are not included in this review and should be reviewed separately after completion.
