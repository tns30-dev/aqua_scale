| Title | AquaShield Monitoring v2 |
| Subtitle | Helping aquaculture farms catch water-quality problems before they cost a harvest. |
| Bullet 1 | Fish, shrimp, and crab farmers monitor pond water by hand a few times a day — missed pH crashes, oxygen drops, or temperature spikes can wipe out an entire pond overnight. |
| Bullet 2 | A single bad pond costs thousands of dollars in lost stock, plus the operational time spent trying to recover it. |
| Bullet 3 | Our platform watches every pond continuously, raises alerts the moment readings drift out of safe range, and lets operators compare ponds side by side. |
| Bullet 4 | Built for aquaculture operators running multiple ponds across shrimp, fish, and crab hatchery profiles. |
| Bullet 5 | Delivers earlier detection, fewer stock losses, less manual labour, and data-backed farm decisions. |
