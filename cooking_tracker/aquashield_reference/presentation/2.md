## Speaker notes (≈ 60s — say out loud, don't put on slide)

> "Here's what the system *does* at a glance. There are two kinds of users, and the use case diagram captures both.

> **The Farm Manager** is the operational user — the person who actually runs the farm day to day. They sign in, look at live pond data, get alerts when readings go out of safe range, review what happened over the last week, and compare ponds side by side to see what's working. They also evaluate whether treatments are improving water quality. Essentially: everything you'd do if you were trying to *keep your fish alive and growing.*

> **The System Administrator** is the configuration user — the person who sets the platform up before the farm manager ever logs in. They define what a 'shrimp farm' or a 'crab hatchery' looks like, create projects and ponds, register IoT devices, declare which sensors measure which parameters, set the safe thresholds, and manage who has access to what.

> So the scope you're looking at is: **one operations workflow** and **one administration workflow** — and the rest of the deck shows how we built both."
