## Speaker notes (≈ 75–90s — say out loud, don't put on slide)

> "Each sprint is one week — about eighty hours of focused work as the baseline. Every sprint had a planned budget and a tracked actual.

> The total project cost **775 hours** across 8 sprints — **19 percent over** the original 650-hour estimate.

> Sprint 1 covered roadmap milestone 1 — stabilising the legacy code. Overran 20 percent because the legacy bug surface was larger than first-pass triage suggested.

> Sprints 2, 3, and 4 covered roadmap milestones 2 and 3 — the design phase. Each sprint overran 10 to 19 percent because mentor-feedback iterations added passes on the use case, the ERD, and the architecture diagrams. That's a *good* overrun — catching design issues early is cheaper than catching them in code.

> Sprint 5 was milestone 4 — User Management with Project and Profile configuration. It overran 22 percent because User Management itself went through three implementation passes per consultant feedback.

> Sprint 6 was milestone 5 — the operational features, including the Digital Twin refactor. Overran 17 percent, mostly on the legacy Digital Twin work; fresh builds — Treatment, Alerts, Visualisation — were on-budget.

> Sprint 7 was the security half of milestone 7 — HttpOnly cookies, CSRF, input validation. Came in close to estimate because it was a known checklist.

> Sprint 8 — the current sprint at 125 hours — is the densest. It bundles three roadmap milestones: Pond Comparison from milestone 6, the CI/CD pipeline with IaC and GCP demo from milestone 7, and the MQTT IoT pipeline from milestone 8. That's where the integration cost lands, and it's where the live demo runs from."

## Insight — patterns the panel will likely ask about

- **Design vs build overruns** — Design sprints (2, 3, 4) had small overruns (~12–19%) driven by review feedback. Build sprints (5, 6, 8) had larger overruns (~17–25%) driven by integration complexity. Both are within healthy variance for a research-led project.
- **Why is Sprint 8 the biggest?** — It bundles three roadmap milestones (M6, M7, M8) into the current sprint specifically so the panel sees the *latest* work in the live demo. Production doesn't carry this work yet — it's local-only on the new GCP environment.
- **Why is Sprint 7 the smallest?** — Pure security hardening from a known checklist (HttpOnly, CSRF, input validation, secret strategy); no novel tech, no integration with new modules.
- **What's not in this hour total?** — Roadmap milestones 9 (Notification Service) and 10 (Model Integration) are scoped but not started — they'll appear in the *next* delivery cycle's effort tracker.
