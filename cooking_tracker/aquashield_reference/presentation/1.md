## Slide content (what appears on the slide)

### Title
**AquaShield Monitoring v2**

### Subtitle
*Helping aquaculture farms catch water-quality problems before they cost a harvest.*

### Body — bulleted

- **The problem** — Fish, shrimp, and crab farmers monitor pond water by hand a few times a day. Missed pH crashes, oxygen drops, or temperature spikes can wipe out an entire pond overnight.
- **The cost** — A single bad pond costs the farm thousands of dollars in lost stock, plus the operational time spent trying to recover it.
- **Our solution** — A continuous-monitoring platform that watches every pond, raises alerts the moment readings drift out of safe range, and lets operators compare ponds side by side to learn what's working.
- **Who it's for** — Aquaculture operators running multiple ponds across different stock types (shrimp · fish · crab hatchery).
- **The value** — Earlier detection · fewer stock losses · less manual labour · data-backed decisions on farm operations.

## Speaker notes (≈ 60s, ~150 words — say out loud, don't put on slide)

> "Good morning. I'm presenting **AquaShield Monitoring v2**, an aquaculture monitoring platform I built over eight sprints for my MTech in Software Engineering.

> Aquaculture is a sensitive business — pond conditions can swing in hours, and a missed oxygen drop or pH crash can kill an entire pond's stock overnight. Today, most small and mid-sized farms still rely on manual checks: someone walks the ponds two or three times a day, dips a probe in, writes a number down. Between checks, the farm is blind.

> AquaShield replaces those blind hours with continuous monitoring. The platform watches every pond around the clock, raises alerts the moment readings drift out of safe range, and gives operators a single dashboard to see what's happening across every pond they run.

> The business value is straightforward — fewer stock losses, less manual labour, and data-backed decisions instead of gut-feel ones.

> In the next eight minutes, I'll walk through how we built it."
